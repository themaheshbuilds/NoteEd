User Guidebook & Tour

Learn how to get the most out of NoteEd's AI study tools and features.

🎓 1. Setting up Course & Syllabus (Standard or Custom Degree) ▼

• Pre-configured Branches: In Syllabus Setup, choose your standard degree branch (e.g. B.Tech CSE, IT, ECE) and semester to get official curriculum topics.

• MBA / Degree / Any Course: Switch to the "MBA / Degree / Other" tab to specify your exact degree, semester, and course names. NoteEd automatically creates a 5-unit curriculum with foundational topics for each custom subject!

📝 2. Generating Notes & Solved Math Problems ▼

• Step-by-Step Worked Calculations: Quantitative and mathematics topics automatically generate 6+ fully worked numerical problems with given data, formulas in MathJax LaTeX, and zero skipped arithmetic steps.

• Study Mode: Toggle between Learning Mode (in-depth textbook coverage) and Exam Prep Mode (rapid high-yield formulas and cheat sheets).

• Stop Generation Anytime: If you are satisfied with generated notes, tap "🛑 Stop & Show Generated Content" to immediately save and read your notes without waiting for quizzes.

⚡ 3. Flashcards, Quizzes & Viva Voce ▼

• Active Recall Flashcards: Flip cards and rate your confidence (Easy, Medium, Hard) to optimize your revision schedule.

• Interactive Quizzes: Test your knowledge with timed 5-question exam MCQs and view detailed answer explanations.

• 🎙️ Viva Voce & Oral Q&A: Master tough viva questions, tricky edge cases, and conceptual oral interview inquiries.

💬 4. AI Tutor Chat & Document Q&A ▼

• Click the "💬 AI Chat" button in any topic to ask specific questions, request extra practice problems, or clarify formulas with your dedicated AI tutor.
